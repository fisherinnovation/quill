Local first

Private by design

Audio stays on your computer

Whisper transcribes on your own hardware, on the GPU on Apple Silicon. Recordings are kept as compact FLAC files in a folder you choose.

→

You choose where text goes

Notes are written by a model you pick: through OpenRouter, only to providers that don't store or train on your data, or with Ollama on your own machine, so nothing leaves it at all.

Keys in your OS keychain

API keys live in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager, never in files or logs.

No telemetry

No analytics, no tracking. Logs stay on your computer and never contain what you said.

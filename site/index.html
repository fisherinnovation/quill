Local first

Private by design

Audio stays on your computer

Whisper transcribes on your own hardware, on the GPU on Apple Silicon and on Windows graphics cards. Recordings are kept as compact FLAC files in a folder you choose.

→

You choose where text goes

Notes, live notes and dictation clean-up use a model you pick: through OpenRouter, only to providers that don't store or train on your data, or with Ollama on your own machine, so nothing leaves it at all.

Keys in your OS keychain

API keys live in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager, never in files or logs.

No telemetry

No analytics, no tracking. Besides your model, Quill only downloads speech models and checks for updates once a day. Logs never contain what you said.

Pay for what you use, not a seat

Quill is free. Notes are written by a model you choose, and you pay that provider directly: about 3 cents an hour of meeting with live notes, or nothing with a local model.
